## Full description (max 4000)
DeryCode builds software that runs your business — even when the internet doesn't.

This app is the fastest way to reach us and to run everything we make from one place.

WHAT YOU CAN DO IN THE APP
• Browse every DeryCode product: DeryAccount POS and accounting, School Report Maker, Sageco Evergreen property, Dery Market, SACCO Wallet
• See every price in Ugandan shillings before you call anyone. No "contact us for a quote" games
• Download our other apps straight from the app
• Message us on WhatsApp in one tap
• Join the partner programme and earn 20% commission on software you recommend
• Read our answers to the questions Ugandan businesses actually ask about POS, school systems and property platforms

BUILT FOR UGANDA
• Works on a weak connection, and shows a useful offline page instead of an error when data drops
• Prices published in UGX
• Support on WhatsApp, from the people who wrote the code
• Made in Kyenjojo, Tooro, Uganda

HOW THE PRODUCTS WORK
DeryAccount records sales, stock, customers and books of account on the device itself. School Report Maker lets teachers enter marks on their phones and prints report cards at term end. Sageco Evergreen lists verified property. None of them need the internet to do their core work.

ABOUT DERYCODE TECHNOLOGIES
We are a Ugandan software studio. We build the systems, we operate them, and we support them long after launch.
